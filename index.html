The Final Curtain Falls

12 Years · 2014 — 2026 🎃

Do you remember the first one? Maybe you were nervous — not sure what to expect, not sure how strange it would get. And then you walked through the door and the music was wrong and the lights were wrong and someone was already wearing something that made you laugh until you couldn't breathe. And just like that — you were home.

Twelve years. Twelve Octobers. Twelve nights where the ordinary world was left at the door and something wilder, stranger, and more alive took its place. We have gathered in costumes and in chaos. We have danced badly and laughed loudly and made decisions that were, by any reasonable measure, completely terrible. And every single one of those terrible decisions became a story we still tell.

Think about the people who have stood in that room over the years. Some of you have grown up inside these walls — arrived young and ridiculous and somehow grown into slightly older, slightly more ridiculous versions of yourselves. Some of you have brought new people, watched them become part of this thing, watched friendships form in the dark over drinks and fake blood and whatever unholy game someone dragged in that year. Some of you have shown up through heartbreak, through hard times, through years when you really needed a night where none of it mattered — and this place gave you that. It always did.

There are people who came every single year without fail. People who travelled for this. People who built their costumes for weeks. People who still talk about that one year when that one thing happened — you know the one. We all know the one.

These nights didn't happen because of a venue or a playlist or a theme. They happened because of you. Every single person who ever walked through that door carrying a bottle and a costume and whatever mad energy they had brought with them that year — you are the reason this became something worth remembering. You are the reason it lasted twelve years. You are the reason it deserves a proper goodbye.

And so here we are. The last one. It feels strange to write those words, and stranger still to mean them. But all great things must eventually take their bow, and Clayton Horror House has had a longer, louder, more glorious run than anyone had any right to expect when this all began.

So come one last time. Dress up. Show up. Be ridiculous. Be yourselves. Drink something regrettable. Play something unhinged. Hold the people around you a little tighter than usual, because you know — we all know — that nights like this don't last forever. And this one, specifically, will not come again.

Make it worthy of twelve years of love. Make it a night that echoes. Make it something we carry with us long after the last candle goes out.

And I mean that. Not just show up — but show up. This is the year we make them all look small by comparison. The year people reference for the rest of their lives when they try to explain what Clayton Horror House actually was. Because the people who weren't there won't quite believe it, and the people who were will never forget it. That's what we're making this October. The one. The story that outlasts all the other stories.

And here's how we do it — together. Bring a game. Bought, borrowed, handmade, printed off at midnight, drawn on a napkin — it doesn't matter. What matters is that you bring something to the table, literally. A party is only a party when everyone's in it, when everyone's got skin in the game, when there's something to play and someone to beat and something ridiculous on the line. Every game that walks through that door is another reason for someone to lean in, to laugh, to be present. So bring one. Make it weird. Make it hard. Make it something nobody's played before. That's how we make sure this final night belongs to every single person in the room — not just the loudest, not just the ones who knew each other longest, but everyone. One last night where nobody sits on the sidelines.

From the bottom of my heart — thank you.
Thank you for every year. Every costume. Every ridiculous game.
Every terrible idea you brought with you and every beautiful memory you left behind.

It has been the greatest honour to haunt you. 🖤
